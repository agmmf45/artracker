# Changelog

## 5.0.0 — Life OS (unreleased, branch `life-os`)
Complete rebuild of Daqeeq as a personal operating system (Life · Study · Health · Settings) on React/TypeScript with a local-first, per-record data model.

**Kept from v4:** every feature, plus your data (automatic, repeatable migration; original blob preserved).

**Fixed from v4:** stored XSS between users, all-accounts leak via `/api/users`, force-friending, non-expiring sessions, open AI proxy, password change without current password, unverified Google linking, lost friend requests, savings double-subtraction, orphaned transactions on wallet delete, UTC water dates, Hijri events hard-coded to 2025–26, workouts lost on refresh.

**New:** Overview command center, natural-language quick capture, command palette, inbox, full task manager with recurrence and five views, planner, calendar with drag & drop, ICS and prayer times, routines, goals/projects/milestones, Markdown second brain with backlinks and graph, journal, reviews, study OS (courses → lectures, exams, planner, focus, FSRS flashcards, question bank, dental clinical tracker, research, GPA, analytics), health OS (nutrition, meal planner, sleep, measurements, medication, records, recovery), finance upgrades, XP/levels/achievements/quests/reward shop/garden, English UI, offline PWA, exports.
